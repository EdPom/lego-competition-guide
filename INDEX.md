Building-book contents below are checked against the book covers; book numbers differ from Field Map model numbers; both 45521 and 45522 example tool families are included.

## Version and scope

This index records the official links reviewed on September 21, 2026. It covers the supplied season page’s Future Edition grades 3–8 section, not every regional or account-only resource. Building-book titles were checked against their contents; page counts refer to those versions.

No dedicated Future Edition Game Updates link was listed in that section on the review date. Revisit the [official season page](https://education.lego.com/en-us/first-lego-league/season-materials/#future-edition-3-8) and organizer announcements before an event. The nearby Founders Edition updates concern a different edition.

The Teacher Portal may require an account. The setup guide, session listing, and Introduction facilitation notes were reviewed; the other lessons are indexed, not reviewed in full. Linked interactive tools were not functionally tested.
