## Accessing prompts and presentations

Open a lesson to find its Classroom Presentation and Facilitation Notes. In the Introduction lesson, the notes are a printable web page rather than a direct PDF download:

- [Introduction facilitation notes](https://teach.legoeducation.com/first-lego-league/lesson/introduction/facilitation-notes)
- [Introduction classroom presentation](https://teach.legoeducation.com/first-lego-league/lesson/introduction/presentation)

The setup guide also contains hardware-basics demonstrations for pairing, sensors, motors, and wireless control. It states that each team needs two CS & AI kits in addition to the BIOGLOW materials.

## Sessions for developing strategy ideas

As a suggested starting point, first revisit sessions 04–08 to connect individual roles to combined play. Sessions 10–11 are the most directly relevant entries for generating and testing ideas. Use their prompts to facilitate discussion; let the children decide which design or sequence they want to try.

These are indexed online links. Only the setup guide, session listing, and Introduction facilitation notes were reviewed during collection. The other lesson contents and presentations have not been archived or checked in full. A LEGO account/session may be needed.
