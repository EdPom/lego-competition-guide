# Theoretical score ceiling

[Home](../README.md) · [Official resource index](../INDEX.md)

Calculated September 21, 2026 from the official BIOGLOW Future Edition Game Missions, Scoresheet, Rulebook, and the [starting-token inventory](token-flow-and-scoring.md#2-complete-starting-inventory). This is our mathematical derivation, not an official published maximum or a demonstrated match result.

**Balanced full-field ceiling: 990 points for each team, 1,980 combined.** This assumes unlimited execution time, successful legal tool actions, no penalties or lost tokens, all optional invasive pieces included and contained, and both teams coordinating. It also assumes that the fourth K used to trigger each queen can be legally recovered afterward while three K remain in that forest and the queen scoring condition remains satisfied. That recovery has not yet been demonstrated on a working model.

## One allocation giving 990 each

| Scoring item | Allocation / condition per team | Points per team |
|---|---|---:|
| Microbiome placement | 3 K in each of 3 microbiomes: 9 K retained | 180 |
| Young forest bonus | At least 3 K remain; opposite queen defeated | 40 |
| Canopy | 15 R cycled up and retained | 150 |
| Waterfall | 50 genuine counted passages | 250 |
| Nest and hollow tree | All 3 nest K and both hollow-tree R released | 50 |
| Optional invasive setup | All 5 optional pieces added | 100 |
| Containment | 5 optional pieces plus the required queen | 60 |
| Shared haven | 32 tokens across the full field, worth 160 to each team | 160 |
| **Total** | | **990** |

The full field starts with **40 K + 40 R**. Across both teams, 18 K remain in microbiomes and 30 R remain in canopies. That leaves **22 K + 10 R = 32 haven tokens**. Each team can contribute 11 K and 5 R; both receive the full 160-point shared score. There are no R left in the tree bases in this allocation.

Waterfall passages and stash releases happen before final placement. They do not consume their tokens. Repeated waterfall use is our interpretation of Mission 3, as documented in the [waterfall evidence and interpretation](token-flow-and-scoring.md#5-what-has-been-demonstrated-and-what-remains-open). The fourth forest K can likewise serve as a temporary trigger and then move elsewhere, if legal recovery is physically achievable.

**If the fourth K remains in each forest:** only 30 tokens reach the haven, making this allocation **980 each / 1,960 combined**. If only one extra K remains, the shared haven loses 5 points for each team. This is a new recovery experiment to test, not a claim that the rules require four K to remain.

## Why 1,980 is the combined upper bound

Count each shared haven token as 10 combined points: 5 to each team.

- **K placement:** at most 18 K across the field earn 20 each in microbiomes, and the other 22 earn at most 10 each in the haven: **580 combined**.
- **R placement:** each of the 40 R earns at most 10 combined points, either in a canopy or in the shared haven: **400 combined**. Base placement earns only 5 combined points per R.
- **Other scoring:** both forest bonuses (80), both waterfall caps (500), both nest/hollow releases (100), all optional setup pieces (200), and all 12 invasive pieces contained (120): **1,000 combined**.

Thus **580 + 400 + 1,000 = 1,980**. The allocation above attains this arithmetic bound with equal scores. A small exhaustive enumeration of retained K (0–9 per team) and canopy R (0–15 per team), assigning remaining tokens to the haven, also gives 990 as the largest equal score. This validates the token accounting, not physical execution or fitting all tokens into scoring areas.

An interesting consequence: canopy placement and haven placement both contribute 10 to the combined total per R. Canopy placement gives all 10 to one team; haven placement gives 5 to each. Multiple coordinated allocations can therefore have the same combined maximum.

## This is not an absolute ceiling for one team in isolation

Because the haven is shared, the opposing team's choices change our available score. For example, if we retain 9 K and 15 R, while the other team ultimately contributes all 40 of its tokens to the haven, the haven contains 56 tokens. Our total would be **830 + 280 = 1,110**, assuming our other scoring conditions are met. The other team sacrifices its final token-placement points in that example.

That is an illustrative asymmetric allocation, **not a claim that 1,110 is the absolute individual maximum under every possible cross-field arrangement**. Establishing such a maximum would also require resolving any proposed transfers of invasive pieces and the interference implications. For planning where both teams achieve their own missions, use **990 each as the ideal balanced ceiling**, with the fourth-K recovery assumption explicit.
